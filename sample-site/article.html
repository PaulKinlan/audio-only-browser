How might a modern Lighthouse work with large language models?

Published by Paul Kinlan on 3 July 2026; reading time on the source: 18 minutes.

Lighthouse is one of the most successful developer tools we've shipped, but its ability to help the developer ecosystem is capped by the number of tests people can encode.

The essay asks: what if an audit were a sentence? A person could describe in plain English what good looks like and let a model work out how to check it.

The loop

The described web-uplift loop explores important pages and flows, judges them against principles, fixes source when it is available, and then re-audits. Its evidence primitives use the Chrome DevTools Protocol to gather material such as screenshots, traces, DOM data and heap snapshots.

The central argument is to write the quality bar and evals rather than hand-code every audit, while still having a human inspect model-produced edits.
